When Maya entered the bookshop, a sign caught her eye: «Every book has a story». «Does that include this notebook?» she asked, pointing to a blue cover. The owner nodded. «Open it near the middle», he suggested. Maya found a drawing of the village bridge and a note: «Meet me here when the rain stops». The owner placed an old photograph beside the page. «Those are my grandparents», he explained. Maya compared the faces with the drawing and wondered how much of the view would still be recognisable.

The following morning, Maya returned with her sketchbook. «May I borrow the notebook?» she asked. «Bring it back before closing», the owner replied, drawing a map. He marked a bench beside the river. «Start there; the view is better», he advised. At the bridge, a gardener was repairing a sign. «Watch the slippery steps», he warned. Maya thanked him and unfolded the map. «Is that the old mill?» she asked. The gardener pointed across the water. «Yes, just beyond those trees». Maya walked carefully down to the bench and opened her sketchbook.

Maya spent an hour drawing before returning to the shop. «The trees have grown, but the bridge is the same», she reported. The owner laid her sketch beside the photograph. «You have added a new page to the story», he said. Maya opened the notebook. «May I leave a message?» she asked. «Write something for the next visitor», he suggested. Beneath her drawing she wrote: «Take your time; the map does not show everything». At the door, she noticed a carving above the frame. «Perhaps that will be my next drawing», she said before leaving.
