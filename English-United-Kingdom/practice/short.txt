The baker left a note on the door: «Back in five minutes».

When he returned, a neighbour was waiting with a basket. «There is no hurry», she said. They had planned to share breakfast.

Before leaving, Emma asked, «Should we take an umbrella?» Her brother looked at the clouds and replied, «Better take two».

At the park, a musician asked, «May I shelter my violin?» They lent him one of their umbrellas.

At the market, Leo pointed to some apples. «These are for a pie», he said.

The seller added one more to his bag. «Something for the walk home», she explained.

The teacher wrote «Questions help us learn» on the board. Someone at the back raised a hand.

«Can we start with the part we do not understand?» That was how the lesson began.

Grandad opened the toolbox and said, «First we must find the missing screw».

His granddaughter pointed at the floor. «I think it is underneath your shoe». They both started laughing.

The sign said, «Ten minutes to the viewpoint».

After climbing the hill, Eva remarked, «It did not mention the steps, but the view is worth it». They stopped there for lunch.

As he delivered the letter, the postman asked, «Does someone here play the piano?»

A voice answered from inside, «Yes, but today we are learning how to enjoy a little silence».

On the last page of his notebook, Ben wrote, «Tomorrow I will try again».

Then he added a smaller sentence underneath: «But this time I will begin after breakfast».
